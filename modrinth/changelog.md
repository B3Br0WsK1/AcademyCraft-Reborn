# 1.0.0-beta.1 (Minecraft 26.3)

First public beta of AcademyCraft Reborn — a full port of AcademyCraft 1.1.3 from Forge 1.12.2 to Fabric.

- All 4 categories and every skill ported
- Ability Developer, skill tree, presets, data terminal and all apps
- Machines, wireless energy network, worldgen, advancements, tutorial
- Fixes compared to the original: Electron Bomb now reliably hits the target; Railgun coin QTE no longer fails when the key is pressed early; coin and railgun charge effects render correctly in third person
- Removed: analytics, IC2 / RF / JEI / CraftTweaker integrations

Known issues: visual differences in some effects (custom shaders were replaced); multiplayer is lightly tested.
